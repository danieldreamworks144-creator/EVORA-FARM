Our Story

Rooted in Tradition,
Home to Animals & Plants

For over a century, Golden Acres has been a living tapestry of pasture and field. Our animals graze freely on nutrient-rich grasses, while our crops thrive in soil enriched by their presence. It's a circle of life that nourishes both land and family.

The Journey

A Century in the Making

1892

The First Seed & First Calf

Elias Hartman plants the first wheat field and brings home the first dairy cow, laying the foundation for a mixed farm.

1935

Through the Dust

Against all odds, the Hartman family survives the Dust Bowl by diversifying crops and keeping animals that enriched the soil.

1968

A New Generation

The third generation introduces rotational grazing and organic practices, creating a symbiotic cycle between plants and animals.

2005

Community Roots

Golden Acres opens its doors with the first annual Harvest Festival, celebrating both the animals and the harvest.

2024

Today & Beyond

Now a thriving ecosystem of livestock and crops, Golden Acres continues to innovate while honoring the legacy of those who came before.
